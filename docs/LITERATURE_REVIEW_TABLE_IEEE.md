| **2025** | Vibina [11] | *Deep CNN-Driven Automated Lung Disease Identification* | Deep CNN classification pipeline for multi-class pulmonary lesions | Dependent on single-center datasets and pure convolutional feature extractors that suffer from limited receptive fields; lacks self-attention mechanisms and predictive confidence calibration. |

### Literature Synthesis & Analysis

Foundational studies conducted in 2024 established core benchmarks and architectural trade-offs in computer-aided thoracic diagnosis. Yaligar *et al.* [16] benchmarked dense residual convolutional architectures across multi-label disorders, revealing that while deep CNNs achieve high diagnostic AUCs, localized convolutional kernels struggle to model long-range spatial correlations across bilateral lung fields. Concurrently, Barra *et al.* [15] evaluated explainable AI techniques across clinical radiography, demonstrating that gradient-weighted saliency methods (Grad-CAM) provide computationally viable heatmaps compared to computationally prohibitive perturbation frameworks such as LIME and SHAP.

Throughout 2025, investigations focused on evaluating Vision Transformers alongside convolutional models. Pure transformer models such as CXR-ViT by Kansal *et al.* [4] and large-scale benchmarks by Ghosh *et al.* [5] proved that patch-level self-attention excels at capturing diffuse thoracic patterns (such as cardiomegaly and pulmonary edema) that local convolutions struggle to aggregate. To combine local textural inductive bias with global contextual embeddings, Rafi *et al.* [6] and Naghash *et al.* [9] (HyCoViT) demonstrated that hybrid CNN-ViT architectures mitigate overfitting in data-constrained medical regimes. Furthermore, attention-enhanced CNNs like CBAM-DenseNet121 [8], DAViT [10], Vibina [11], Qi [13], abnormality-guided report generation [14], and explainability frameworks like layer-wise Grad-CAM [3], [7], [12] enhanced localized sensitivity and clinician trust.

Most recently in 2026, research prioritized holistic diagnostic frameworks. Shukla *et al.* [1] confirmed that contrast enhancement and CLAHE preprocessing directly govern classification fidelity across diverse scanner sources. Altun and Candemir [2] demonstrated that differential evolution-guided feature combinations between convolutional and self-attention representations achieve superior thoracic pathology discrimination compared to standalone backbones.

### Identified Research Gaps
1. **Resolution and High-Frequency Loss**: Most existing models downsample radiographs to $224 \times 224$, losing subtle apical nodules and fine reticular opacities.
2. **Heuristic Fusion vs. Relational Conditioning**: Fusion strategies often rely on simple late concatenation without explicitly modeling cross-attention and inter-disease relational dependencies.
3. **Absence of Calibrated Uncertainty in Diagnostic Workflows**: High-accuracy models routinely omit predictive uncertainty bounds, treating confident and ambiguous predictions identically.
4. **Lack of Dual-Modality Quantitative XAI Auditing**: Visual explanations are rarely audited quantitatively across paired CNN and ViT modalities on identical test cohorts.

### How the Proposed RESPIRA Framework Resolves These Gaps
The proposed **RESPIRA** framework addresses these limitations through:
1. **Deterministic Preprocessing Pipeline**: CLAHE enhancement alongside Otsu morphological lung field masking and anatomical fallback heuristics.
2. **Dual-Backbone Hybrid Architecture**: EfficientNet-B0 (local textural inductive bias) coupled with Vision Transformer ViT-B/16 (global thoracic context).
3. **High-Resolution Multi-Modal Fusion**: High-resolution ($512 \times 512$) weighted probability fusion (0.35 EfficientNet + 0.65 ViT) alongside an advanced 5-stage latent feature fusion pipeline with disease-conditioned query attention.
4. **Calibrated Uncertainty Quantification**: Normalized binary entropy and decision margin uncertainty combined into a 3-tier clinical risk triage rule (Low Risk, Moderate Review, Mandatory Senior Audit).
5. **Paired Dual-Modality Explainability Audit**: Systematic quantitative concordance audit comparing Grad-CAM and ViT Cross-Attention Mapping across 1,947 held-out test radiographs (achieving a 93.73% cross-modality agreement rate).
6. **Full Clinical Platform Implementation**: Authenticated asynchronous FastAPI backend, SQLAlchemy ORM, automated ReportLab PDF diagnostic reporting, and a React 19 / Electron desktop suite.
